🌬️ BREATHING BACKGROUNDS - PHONE SETUP 🌬️
================================================

STEP 1: On Your Computer (Mac/Windows/Linux)
================================================
Open Terminal or Command Prompt and paste this:

cd /home/user/Jimmy2 && python3 -m http.server 8000

Leave this window open! You should see:
"Serving HTTP on 0.0.0.0 port 8000"


STEP 2: Get Your Computer's IP Address
================================================
Open a NEW Terminal/Command Prompt and paste:

hostname -I

You'll see a number like: 192.168.1.105
Write this down!


STEP 3: On Your Phone (iOS or Android)
================================================
Open your phone's browser and go to:

http://[YOUR-IP]:8000

Replace [YOUR-IP] with the number from Step 2
Example: http://192.168.1.105:8000


STEP 4: Add to Home Screen
================================================

iOS (iPhone):
- Tap the Share button (arrow at bottom)
- Tap "Add to Home Screen"
- Name it "Breathing"
- Tap "Add"

Android:
- Tap the three dots menu (top right)
- Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
- Confirm


TROUBLESHOOTING:
================================================
❌ "Page won't load"
   → Make sure computer and phone are on SAME WiFi
   → Make sure Terminal window is still open
   → Try: http://192.168.1.XXX:8000 (check IP again)

❌ "Can't find IP address"
   → Try hostname -I (Mac/Linux) or ipconfig (Windows)
   → Look for line starting with 192.168 or 10.0
